Advisory areas

Learnings I can share

A few example focus areas to help teams chart a smoother path ahead, clear of common pain points

Verification strategy

Quality checks for phased releases

Defining metrics for intermediate project stages with backward-compatibility checks, sign-off criteria, selective deploying of formal verification, gradual build-up of test cases helps pace team activities with a positive momentum.

AI adoption in verification

Time-tested old school - always reliable

Enabling AI-assisted log parsing for simulation debugging proved that manually cross verifying misses like an error-free empty log file using a not-yet-robust AI playbook is a will-never-fail traditional best-practice.

IP quality

Post-silicon traceability

Back-tracing post-silicon bug requires root-causing it to specific IP version(s), enhancing tests to detect its recurrence, and then ensuring the corrected, fixed IP version is rolled into all co-affected SoCs in subsequent SoC-revisions.

Program execution

Milestone & risk discipline

Constant active participation in relevant forums is needed to become aware early of potential risks, schedule slips and later escalation of urgent issues to quickly resolve them is a must to minimize the impact to pre-committed milestone timelines.

Toolchain decisions

EDA tool strategy & migration

Adopting a mix of low-maintenance industry-standard tools as well as yet-to-be-re-purposed, high-maintenance, proprietary, custom scripts is needed at times for a low-risk, timely completion of complex automation processes in existing IP release flows

Team & org

Building & onboarding DV teams

Designing cross-site onboarding programs and building a strong, results-driven team-culture goes a long way in connecting teams with unified, shared ownership.

About Mala

A journey through
generations of chips

Mala S Masti

I have worked for nearly 12 years at Qualcomm, San Diego on the MSM modem series — from conception through post-silicon, across 2G, 2.5G, 3G, and early 4G. I have owned several IPs end-to-end - architecting, designing, coding, verifying, documenting and demonstrating live a few IPs to high-stakes customers in real-time lab environments.

I have spent a little over 7.5 years at Intel, Bangalore, running IP programs for over 10 concurrent SoCs at a time across Xeon data server, Core Ultra Client, Graphics, Gaming, DFT, Telemetry and Network Edge SoCs. I have held roles of Design Verification Engineering Manager, Design Automation Engineer and IP Program Manager which helped me gain a close-up view of the gaps between customer expectation and product quality as well as between a technically-driven option and a business-oriented choice. I have thus learned along the way to handle conflicting pressures for defining scope, planning schedule and aligning stakeholders

Additionally I have mentored many university undergraduate and graduate engineering students to get hired as interns and/ or full-time engineers at various hardware product/ service companies in Bangalore.

I now help a few start-ups, sharing what I have learned so far - enabling them to make better, informed decisions.
